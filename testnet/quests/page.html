Latheon testnet quests

Testnet only. LTH is a test token with no value. Nothing here is an offer to sell or buy any token.

Use a separate test wallet for quests: quest platforms link your wallet address to your social profile. Never send anyone the keys of a note (spend key, view key) or your note text.

1. Your one-time code

2. Make a disclosure report

Open the demo, go to the report tab, paste this code as the auditor's one-time code and choose one deposit you made from this address into a current pool (V8 for the V8 quests). Save the report file. Do not include keys, and leave the receipt option off: a receipt would also show us your withdrawal, and we do not accept it here.

3. Upload it

We check: the proof with the chain's disclose verifier, that it was made for your code, that the deposit exists in a current Latheon pool and was sent from your address. We keep only your address, the time and how many rows were V8. We never check withdrawals: that would link your deposit to your withdrawal address.
